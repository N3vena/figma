**Minimalistic Poster – Monster Energy Mango Loco**

This poster is inspired by the Monster Energy Mango Loco drink. The design focuses on simplicity, bold colors and strong visual contrast. The turquoise background combined with vibrant orange details creates an eye-catching and modern look. The can is the main focus of the composition, while the clean layout and minimal typography give the poster a professional advertising style.
Design style:Minimalism / Product Advertising
